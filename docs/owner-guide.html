ColdTrace Quick Guide

Temperature and door monitor for this fridge/freezer. It watches the temperature, sounds an alarm, and sends readings to a web dashboard.

What the lights and sounds mean

Green blip every 5 secondsWorking normally. Nothing to do.
Amber flashing slowlyThe door is open.
Amber flashing fast, short beep every 5 secondsThe door has been open over 1 minute. Close it.
Red flashing and buzzerToo warm for longer than the alarm delay (15 minutes unless changed). See "If the red alarm sounds".
Red flashing slowly, silentThe alarm was muted, but it is still too warm.
No light at allNo power or a fault. Check the plug and call for help.

The mute button

  • Press: silence the buzzer for 15 minutes.
  • Hold for 3 seconds: silence for 12 hours.
  • Press again while muted: turn the alarm back on.
  • The screen shows a speaker icon with the time left.
  • After power-up it stays muted for 15 minutes.

The screen

  • Large number: current temperature in °C.
  • Top left: WiFi icon (connected) and SD card icon (recording).
  • Top right: the clock.
  • No WiFi icon: it keeps recording on the card and sends the missing hours when WiFi returns.

If the red alarm sounds

  • Make sure the door is fully closed and the unit is running (compressor on, not unplugged).
  • Check the food with a hand thermometer. Do not rely on the display alone.
  • If it stays warm, move the food to another cold unit and call for service.

Please don't

  • Unplug the unit, cover the sensor, or let the probe cable get pinched in the door seal.
  • Get the case wet, or put it inside the fridge or freezer. Only the thin probe goes inside.
